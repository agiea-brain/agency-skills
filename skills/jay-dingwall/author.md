Jay Dingwall has run FamousFolks, a Toronto strategic creative consultancy, since 2006, working with growth-stage B2B SaaS companies whose brand and messaging have fallen behind the product they have built. The firm covers positioning, rebrands, campaigns, sales enablement and experience design under one roof, which puts Jay in the account conversations where existing clients turn into larger ones. An MBA-educated strategist with two decades in branding and entrepreneurship, he is currently focused on the systems behind the consultancy itself: pipeline, process, and making good work convert into predictable growth. His skills encode how he reads an existing account for the work it should already be buying.
