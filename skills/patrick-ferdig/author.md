Patrick Ferdig founded The Power of Partnering, a Denver consultancy that helps agencies, integrators and B2B technology providers partner better to grow faster. Over more than 20 years in enterprise software he built and led partner programs at hybris, commercetools and Magnolia, scaling ecosystems from zero to more than 60 active partners and driving over $20M in partner-sourced revenue. He now applies AI and automation to partner operations, as an AI strategist with AchieveUnite and GTM leader at HitWit, and builds his own fixes for LinkedIn's gaps in tracking signals and business development. His skills turn the public signals around clients, prospects and partners into a reason to reach out.
