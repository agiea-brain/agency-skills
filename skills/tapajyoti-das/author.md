Tukan Das co-founded Gia, where agencies and consulting firms turn their own frameworks and judgment into a system they can deploy inside a client's environment. He previously co-founded and sold LeadSift, which served several hundred customers before being acquired by Foundry in 2021, and his view of this market comes from several hundred conversations with the owners of boutique agencies and consulting firms, first while deciding what to build and again while rebuilding it around what he calls Always On Consulting. His skills encode the parts of winning and positioning work that depend on hearing what a prospect actually said and writing it back to them without flattening it into category language.
