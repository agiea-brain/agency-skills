Paul Xue founded Spacestation Labs, a Toronto agency that helps brands show up and win on Reddit through organic content, paid ads and reputation management. He spent years as a software developer and CTO, cofounding five startups, before switching to marketing: he started posting on Reddit after nearly twenty years of lurking, reached 50,000 karma and dozens of viral posts within six months, and grew the agency to more than 40 clients. His skills encode the research that has to happen before a brand posts anything on Reddit, built on what real users actually say rather than what the brand assumes.
