# The thing that changed

A win-back needs one. Without it the message is a check-in, and a check-in to someone who left is an email neither side enjoys.

## The test

One sentence, specific, and true. If it needs two sentences or a qualifier, it is not a change, it is a repositioning of the same thing.

| Not a change | A change |
|---|---|
| "We've grown and added capabilities" | "We now do the implementation as well, which is the part you had to hand to someone else" |
| "We'd love to reconnect" | "We've dropped the minimum engagement to a size that would have fit your budget" |
| "Lots of exciting things happening" | "Priya joined us from your industry and is running that work now" |
| "Checking in to see how you're doing" | "The thing you flagged as the dealbreaker is fixed, and here is how" |

## Kinds that work

**The offer.** Something is now in scope that was not, usually the piece they had to solve elsewhere.

**The price or the shape.** A smaller engagement, a different commitment, a phased option. This is the one that speaks to everyone who left on budget, which is usually the largest group.

**A capability or a person.** A hire with their industry, a skill the firm did not have. Specific and verifiable.

**Something fixed.** The exact thing they left over. The strongest and the most uncomfortable to send, which is why it rarely gets sent.

**Something in their world.** A regulation, a market shift, a competitor move that makes the old problem urgent again. This one is about them rather than the firm and is often the easiest to open with.

## Kinds that do not work

- Growth. Nobody has ever returned to a firm because it got bigger.
- Awards, rebrands, new websites.
- A new framework with a name. The name is for the firm's benefit, not theirs.
- "We've been thinking about you." Possibly true, not a reason to reply.

## Matching the change to the departure

A single change rarely speaks to everyone who left. Map it before writing anything.

| They left because | Speaks to them |
|---|---|
| Budget | Price or shape |
| Scope gap, went elsewhere for part of it | Offer |
| Champion left | Nothing about the firm. This is a fresh sale. |
| Took it in house | Their world, or how in-house is going |
| Work did not land | Something fixed, named directly |
| Bad fit | Nothing. Leave them. |

Where the change does not speak to a group, they are not on this round's list. Waiting for a change that does is better than sending something that does not.

## Timing

Three to eighteen months after they left is the window. Sooner is too raw; much later and the relationship has decayed to a cold contact with history.

Outside that window, treat it as warm outreach rather than a win-back, and lead with the person rather than the engagement.
