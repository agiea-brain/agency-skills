# Calibration pairs

Read these before the first rewrite. They set the size of the cut: sentences usually lose a third or more, while labels and buttons can gain a word or two when that makes them specific. The examples use agency copy, so match the size of the cut, not the register. The numbers are illustrative; never carry one into real copy.

**Homepage hero.** Before: "We partner with ambitious agencies to develop comprehensive strategies that drive sustainable growth and help you stand out in an increasingly crowded marketplace." After: "Your agency does great work that sounds exactly like everyone else's. We fix the second part."

**Cold email opener.** Before: "Hi Sarah, I hope this email finds you well. I wanted to reach out because I've been following your agency's journey and I'm really impressed with what you've built." After: "Hi Sarah, your homepage says 'full-service digital solutions,' and so do six competitors'."

**LinkedIn hook.** Before: "One thing I've noticed after working with dozens of agency owners over the years is that there tends to be a common pattern in how they price their services." After: "Underpriced agencies all set rates the same way: market minus 10%."

**CTA block.** Before: "Ready to take the next step? We'd love to learn more about your agency and explore whether we might be a good fit. Schedule a no-obligation discovery call today." After: "One call. You leave with a rewritten homepage headline, whether we work together or not. Book the teardown."

**Opening line of a long piece.** Before: "This week I want to dig into something that comes up constantly: the hidden costs of scope creep and what it's doing to your margins." After: "A client showed me his P&L last week, and scope creep was his third-biggest expense. He just didn't have a line item for it."

**Pricing tier.** Before: "Growth Package (Most Popular)." After: "Fill Your First 5 Roles."

**CTA button.** Before: "Learn More." After: "Get the pricing math."

**Subject line.** Before: "Some thoughts on agency pricing." After: "Your retainer is priced off the wrong number."
