# The hook toolbox

Open this for the rewrite (step 6) and the alternates (step 7). Put the straight message into the pattern. Never start from the pattern. Use one per draft, and a different one for each alternate.

1. **The Priced Problem.** Name the problem and what it costs. "That 'flexible scope' clause cost you $60K last year."
2. **The Named Enemy.** Give a plain name to the thing doing damage to what they care about. "Free revisions are eating your margin."
3. **The Contrarian Flat Statement.** The thing they're proud of is the real problem. "Your referrals are the reason you can't raise prices."
4. **The Damning Question.** Ask when proof of health last happened. "When did a stranger last pay you full price?"
5. **The Fork.** Do the thing, or keep suffering the consequence. "Raise your prices or keep funding your clients' margins."
6. **The Receipt.** A specific outcome plus a surprising constraint. "$40K retainer, closed on the first call, no proposal."
7. **The Uncomfortable Mirror.** A time, and the thing they never admit. "It's 9pm and you're rewriting a junior's deck again."
8. **The If/Then Filter.** If they have the symptom, this is for them. "If your best salesperson is the founder, your agency has a ceiling."
9. **The Before/After Compression.** From the painful state to the desired one. "From pitch decks to a waitlist."
10. **The Odd Specific.** An odd number, its units, and a surprising claim. "Three sentences on your homepage decide whether the sales call happens."
11. **The Familiar Phrase Hijack.** An idiom with one word replaced. "Feast, famine, repeat."
12. **The Concrete Tease.** The specific artifact that produces the outcome. "The one line in our proposals that does all the selling."
13. **The Cold Open Story.** A time marker and a dramatic event. "Two years ago I fired our biggest client on a Tuesday."
14. **The Command CTA.** A verb and the specific thing. "Get the pricing math."
