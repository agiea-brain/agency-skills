# Buyer read scoring rubric

Open this to score (step 8). Score in a separate pass, pessimistically, taking the lower number when torn. Rate each dimension 0-10, multiply by its weight, divide by 10, and add them up for a total out of 100.

| # | Dimension | Weight | 10 looks like | 6 looks like | 2 looks like |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| BR1 | Persona fidelity | 12 | Named, sized and situated, every attribute traceable | Real but generic in places | "A busy owner," no sourcing |
| BR2 | Evidence traceability | 20 | Every finding cites a source | One or two rest on reasoning | Confident assertions with nothing behind them |
| BR3 | Domain reality | 18 | Every scenario tested, violation found or none stated | Checked loosely | Never asked if it could happen |
| BR4 | Absence detection | 16 | Names what the buyer wouldn't find, including what craft review can't see | Mostly obvious absences | Only critiques what's on the page |
| BR5 | Severity honesty | 12 | Ranked by cost, honest tiers | A mid finding is inflated | Discovery order, or everything urgent |
| BR6 | Actionability | 12 | Each finding names the fix or decision, plus what to cut | Fixes implied, or no cut | Complaints with no path |
| BR7 | Restraint | 10 | Nothing padded, says so if the piece is mostly fine | One or two preferences in costume | Padded to hit a number |

**PASS: 85/100, no dimension below 6, and BR2 at 8 or higher.**

Draft, score, revise the lowest dimensions, then re-score the whole read. Maximum three cycles.

## When a dimension is stuck

- **BR1, BR2 or BR3 stuck** means the source material is too thin. Stop revising, deliver the read marked not done, and ask for the transcript or the missing ICP section. Reasoning around the hole is the fabrication this skill exists to prevent.
- **BR4 stuck** usually means the piece has no significant absences. Say that and deliver a short read.
- **BR7 stuck** means findings are writing preferences. Drop them and shorten.
