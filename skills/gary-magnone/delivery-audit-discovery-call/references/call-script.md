# Delivery audit call script

30 minutes. The owner talks; you ask and count. Bracketed text is what you fill in from their answers.

## Block timings

| Block | Existing customer | Net new or closed-lost |
|---|---|---|
| Open empty | 0-2 | 0-3 |
| Set the scene | skip | 3-6 |
| Find the pain | inside the open | 6-10 |
| Anchor the activity's value | 2-5 | 10-13 |
| Vertical drill (one pain) | 5-15 | 13-22 |
| Magic wand | 15-22 | 22-26 |
| Pricing | 22-27 | 26-29 |
| Lock + referral | 27-30 | 29-30 |

The net-new call has no usage data to lean on, so the scene-set matters more. Keep it tight: 3 minutes maximum.

## Open empty

Existing customer:

- "What were your thoughts coming into this call? What was on your mind when you saw 'delivery audit' on the invite?"
- (If they deflect) "When I say client delivery, what comes up for you?"
- (If still nothing) "What's the part of delivery you wonder if AI should be able to do already?"

Closed-lost or past conversation:

- "Last time we talked it was about [the old topic]. We've since shifted toward [your current focus] for firms like yours. Curious what was on your mind agreeing to this call?"
- "When I say challenges or bottlenecks in client delivery, what's the first thing that comes to mind?"

Net new, after one sentence of context on what you do and why you are talking to firm owners about delivery:

- "So I want to keep this open. When I say client delivery, what comes up for you?"

Then shut up. Three full seconds. Silence does the work.

## Set the scene (net new only)

- "Just so I have context, walk me through what your firm actually does. What's a typical engagement look like?"
- "How big is the team doing the delivery work?"
- "How many active engagements right now?"

Listening for: firm size, delivery model, where the leverage might be. Move fast.

## Find the pain (net new only)

- "When you think about the work your team actually does day to day, what's the part you wonder if AI should be able to do already?"
- (If vague) "What's the part of delivery that keeps you up at night right now?"
- (If still vague) "Tell me about the last project where something broke or took way longer than it should have."

You need them to name a specific pain by minute 10. Do not move on without it.

## Anchor the activity's value

- "Just to make sure I'm tracking, [the activity] is a pretty critical part of your delivery, right? This is what clients are actually paying for?"
- "So this is a competitive advantage piece for you?"

Get the yes before moving on.

## Vertical drill (one pain only)

- "Walk me through the process. As nitpicky as you can. You sit down to do [the thing]. Walk me through start to finish."
- "Of all those steps, which one eats the most time?"
- "Like is that 80% of the work? 50%? Just rough."
- "How often does this come up? Per client? Per week?"
- "Who's doing it? You? Someone else?"
- "What's their loaded cost roughly?"
- "Have you tried to solve this already? What did you do?"
- "Why didn't that work?"

Collect numbers. Do not do the math out loud yet.

## Magic wand

- "Let's say this was completely taken care of. The [biggest step] is just done for you. What does your day look like now?"
- "What does that mean in actual numbers? What's a new client worth to you per month?"
- "If you could hire someone, what does that unlock revenue-wise?"
- (If they go small) "That's huge. But would that translate to taking on more work? Or freeing up headspace to fix something else?"

Leave this block with: an annual dollar figure for the pain, an opportunity-cost number, and their words on why it matters.

## Pricing

See pricing-frame.md. Wait for them to ask; prompt at minute 24 (existing) or 27 (net new) if they have not.

## Lock + referral

If yes-ish:

- "I'll send a one-page scope by [tomorrow morning] with the build, pricing, and timeline. Can we put 30 minutes on the calendar later this week to walk through it?"

If maybe:

- "I'll send a summary including the rough numbers. Sit with it. If it makes sense, we scope it properly."

Always:

- "Last thing, totally separate. If anyone comes to mind, another firm owner you respect wrestling with similar stuff, I'd love an intro. Trying to talk to as many of these folks as possible right now."

## Don't forget

- One pain only. Go vertical, not horizontal.
- Anchor the value before digging into the pain.
- The magic wand question is the unlock.
- Price on the call. Never by email.
- Lock the next step before they leave.
