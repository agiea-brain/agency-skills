Kristy Busija founded Next Conversation Consulting in Charlotte in 2017, a woman-owned management consulting and coaching firm that designs and delivers leadership training, coaching and culture work for organizations. She has spent more than twenty years leading talent management and organizational development, holds a master's in industrial and organizational psychology, is an ICF and Board Certified coach, and wrote the best-selling book Hidden in Plain Sight. Her skills encode the recurring work of running a small consulting practice: preparing training decks a facilitator can actually run, prospecting locally without sounding like a sales sequence, and publishing every week without repeating herself.
