Garrett Jestice advises full-time B2B consultants, fractionals and micro-agency owners on offer-market fit: building one repeatable offer around one kind of client, instead of quoting every project from scratch. He spent a decade in B2B software marketing, as Head of Marketing at Lucidpress and then CMO at Eddy, before going solo as a fractional CMO and hitting the same ceiling he now helps clients break. He runs 10x Solo, a private community for experienced B2B service providers, and publishes a weekly newsletter on how solo firms find and keep the right clients. His skills encode the parts of that work he runs on a fixed cadence, starting with how he turns a week of client and prospect calls into content worth publishing.
