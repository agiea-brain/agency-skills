# Scoring rubric

Score every client on four signals. Each signal takes one of four scores: -3, -1, +1 or +3. Client totals run from -12 to +12.

## The four signals

| Signal | The question | What it catches |
|---|---|---|
| **Pay** | Did they pay well for the effort it took? | Margin: price minus scope creep, haggling and slow pay |
| **Energy** | Were they easy and energizing to serve? | The drain: process fit, how many people sign off, revision loops |
| **Proof** | Did they get a result they'd vouch for? | The outcome and their willingness to say so, the raw material for marketing |
| **Fit** | Do they match the business you want to build? | Direction: the right kind of client, the role you want, a shape you can repeat |

## Why the scale is signed

- **A bad client scores below zero.** On a 1-5 scale the worst client is "low positive," and that's false. A drain client uses capacity a best client would have used. Saying yes to a bad fit is saying no to a good one.
- **Four anchors stay easy to tell apart.** A roster of 5-15 clients doesn't hold more resolution than that, and wider scales are fake precision.
- **No zero forces a call.** Fence-sitting is how self-scored tools fail.

## The rubric

Keep the level names on every copy. A roster that reads Thin, Drain, Silent, Adjacent tells the story before anyone adds a number.

### Pay: how well they paid for the effort it took

| Score | Level | Definition |
|---|---|---|
| -3 | Loss | Haggled the price; scope grew past the agreement; paid late; little or no margin left |
| -1 | Thin | Paid the invoice, but margin was low for the hours; only repeatable at a higher price |
| +1 | Fair | Paid at or near list; scope mostly held; acceptable margin for the effort |
| +3 | Full | Paid list without pushback; scope held start to finish; best margin on the roster |

### Energy: how easy they were to serve

| Score | Level | Definition |
|---|---|---|
| -3 | Drain | Worked inside their process; several stakeholders on every signoff; deliverables reopened for revisions |
| -1 | Heavy | More meetings, check-ins and hand-holding than the work needed |
| +1 | Smooth | Ran through your normal process with normal friction; nothing to flag |
| +3 | Energizing | One empowered contact; fast approvals; your process end to end; work you want more of |

### Proof: did they get a result they'd vouch for

| Score | Level | Definition |
|---|---|---|
| -3 | Miss | The result fell short and they know it; wouldn't take a reference call |
| -1 | Silent | Satisfied but quiet; no result captured, no vouch requested; or too soon to tell |
| +1 | Happy | Told you it worked; one concrete result you can point to |
| +3 | Advocate | Would rehire today; gave a testimonial, reference or referral, or would on request |

### Fit: how well they match the business you want to build

| Score | Level | Definition |
|---|---|---|
| -3 | Misfit | Outside your ICP; work you don't want more of; forces you into a role you're leaving |
| -1 | Adjacent | Edge of your ICP; a one-off or work you're phasing out; stays dependent on you |
| +1 | Aligned | The right kind of client and work; mostly the role you want; a shape you can repeat |
| +3 | Cornerstone | Dead-center ICP; work you want to be known for; the role you want; the one to clone |

## Two calls people get wrong

- **Proof measures willingness, not occurrence.** "Came back, renewed, referred" grades the client on your business model. Project work can't renew for years, and referrals nobody asked for won't come. A client who would take a reference call tomorrow scores +3, even if you'll never invoice them again.
- **Fit grades the engagement against the future, not the past.** Pay, Energy and Proof measure the engagement you had. Fit measures it against the business you're building. It stops the audit from cloning your past instead of your future, and it's why a profitable, pleasant, well-vouched client can still be the wrong one to replicate.
