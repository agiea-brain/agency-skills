# Steps 1 and 2: Ad library research

## Step 1: Client ad library analysis

Analyses the client's own Meta Ad Library to understand their current paid creative: formats, messaging, targeting, creative velocity and available ads. Ends with a gap analysis comparing what the brand says it stands for against what is actually in its ads.

```
Can you do an analysis on {brand}'s Meta Ad Library? The end report should be a doc for internal
review. Please include:
- Format breakdown (video, image, carousel, etc.)
- Distribution between partnership ads and brand ads (partnership ads are identified by "with" and
  a creator handle, or "Brand × creator handle" format)
- Core messaging strategies that are being repeated
- Audience personas they appear to be targeting, based on ad content only
- In-depth analysis of all available ads, up to a maximum of 10
- Round-up of their longest-running ads and a trend summary
- Creative velocity: how many ads are being uploaded, when, and when the last upload was
- Gap analysis: compare the brand's stated messaging priorities from the immersion deck against
  what is actually present in the ad library. Note where the ads align and where they fall short
```

### If there are no active ads

```
{brand} has no active Meta Ad Library data. Please search for any available video or display ad
content on YouTube, TikTok, and Google. Additionally, review their organic social content on
Instagram and TikTok as a proxy for their paid creative direction. Summarise findings using the same
framework (format breakdown, messaging themes, apparent targeting, creative velocity) and note
clearly where data is limited or inferred from organic content rather than paid ads.
```

Output: one doc saved to the client folder, for internal QA review only.

## Step 2: Competitor analysis

Analyses the Meta Ad Libraries of 3 competitor or aspirational brands to find patterns, messaging strategies and opportunities for the client.

Before running: pull the 3 competitors or aspirational brands from the immersion deck and collect their ad library links.

```
Can you do a competitor ad library analysis for {brand}? Please analyse the 3 competitor Meta Ads
libraries. The end report should be a doc for internal review. For each brand, please include:
- Format breakdown
- Distribution between partnership ads and brand ads
- Core messaging strategies being repeated
- Audience personas each brand appears to be targeting, based on ad content only. Describe the
  actual audience type (e.g. skin-conscious millennial, clean beauty buyer), not funnel stages or
  content formats.
- Their 3 longest-running ads
End with a summary across all three brands covering: key consistencies, standout data points, and
how {brand} can use this information strategically.
```

### If a competitor has no active ads

```
{competitor} has no active Meta Ad Library data. Please search for any available video or display ad
content on YouTube, TikTok, and Google. Additionally, review their organic social content on
Instagram and TikTok as a proxy for their paid creative direction. Summarise findings using the same
framework (format breakdown, messaging themes, apparent targeting, creative velocity) and note
clearly where data is limited or inferred from organic content rather than paid ads.
```

Output: one doc saved to the client strategy folder, for internal QA review only.
