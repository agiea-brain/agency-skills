# Step 3: Client-facing audit and proposal

Four parts, run in order in the same chat.

## Part A: Client-facing audit

```
The agency audit is final. Please create a client-facing version of the audit for {brand} as a new
doc or tab. It should be high level and decision-ready: a founder should get it in 5 minutes.
- Drop internal detail: no per-email breakdowns, no data-source notes, no revenue-per-recipient
  tables, no assumptions list.
- Keep: the revenue opportunity with simplified math, the 3-5 key findings with proof, the
  scorecard, recommendations tied to findings, and the 30/60/90 plan with expected outcomes.
- Headlines state the takeaway: "Your welcome flow is losing ~$X/month", not "Welcome Flow".
```

## Part B: Review the client-facing audit

Run this straight after Part A in the same chat. Then read the client version yourself and comment on anything to add, cut or simplify before moving on.

```
Before we build the proposal, review the client-facing audit against the final agency audit:
- Missing: list anything important from the agency audit that isn't in the client version (key
  findings, numbers, recommendations) and should be.
- Too detailed: flag any section a founder couldn't take in at a glance, and summarise it further.
- Consistency: check every number, finding and recommendation matches the agency audit.
- Clarity: check every headline states the takeaway and nothing relies on internal jargon.
Make the changes, then summarise what you added, cut and simplified. If I leave further comments
in the doc, apply them when I say comments are in.
```

## Before Part C

- Confirm the client-facing audit is final.
- Have your pricing model, rates or package prices, payment terms and case studies ready.
- List any services the brand asked about outside the audit.

## Part C: Build the proposal

```
Using the final audit in this conversation, please build a proposal for {brand} as a doc I can
comment on.

Pricing inputs:
- Pricing model: {one-off project + monthly retainer / retainer only / project only}
- Rates or package prices: {e.g. flow builds, email design/copy per email, SMS, strategy}
- Minimum term and payment terms: {e.g. 3-month minimum, 50% upfront}
- Services they asked about outside the audit: {services}
- Case studies: {client, category, result}

Please include:
- What we heard: their goals and priorities
- The opportunity: the revenue range and the math, restated briefly
- Recommendations: each tied to a specific audit finding
- Phased plan (30/60/90 days): deliverables and expected outcome per phase
- Investment: a one-off project (scope, timeline, price, payment terms) and/or a retainer (monthly
  deliverables, price, term). Offer 2 tiers if useful. Show package prices only, never internal rates
- Additional services menu: pricing for anything requested outside the audit
- Case studies matched to their category, channel or problem
- Next steps: the specific ask, with a date

Every line item must trace back to a finding; flag anything that can't be justified by the audit
rather than including it. Compare the investment to the conservative revenue opportunity. Flag any
scope or pricing decision I need to make before this goes out. Then stop for my comments.
```

## Part D: Finalise the proposal

Run once you've left comments in the proposal doc.

```
Comments are in. Apply every comment to the proposal and summarise what changed. Flag any comment
that conflicts with the audit, with your reasoning.
```

## Output

A final client-facing audit doc and a final proposal doc. Both are client deliverables.
