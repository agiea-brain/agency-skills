Kayla Bonnette co-founded Outlandish, a creative agency built around taking brave brands past the ordinary, pairing storytelling with the performance side of digital. The agency's work spans marketing and content strategy, video, social, performance marketing, packaging design and email, for consumer brands including Nike, Feastables, Magic Spoon and TikTok. Her skills encode the parts of that work that have to hold up in front of a client: auditing an account honestly, sizing what is actually at stake, and making the case in a way a founder can check.
