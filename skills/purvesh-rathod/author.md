Purvesh Rathod is the founding software engineer at Gia, where he has built the system that captures how a professional services firm actually works and deploys it into the places that firm's clients already use. He came to Gia from cloud engineering at DeepSense and a computer science degree at Dalhousie, and he has shipped through two full product directions, including the rebuild around deploying a firm's knowledge into client environments with per-client isolation. His skills encode what he has learned about getting undocumented process out of people's heads and into something a system can run.
