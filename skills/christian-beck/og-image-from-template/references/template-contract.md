# The template contract

A template works because everyone agrees on which layers hold content and which hold structure. This is that agreement. Adapt the layer names to the firm's own template, but keep the split.

## Content layers

| Layer | Accepts | Budget | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| `title` | One line of plain text | 55 to 65 characters | The only required layer. Written for the feed, not the page. |
| `eyebrow` | A category, client name or series | 24 characters | Optional. Empty is better than vague. |
| `author` | Name, no title, no credentials | 28 characters | Only if the firm's template ships with it. |
| `bg-image` | A photo or screenshot | n/a | Placed into the existing mask with its existing treatment. |

Everything else in the frame is structure: logo, grid, background shapes, overlays, safe-area guides. Not touched, not moved, not recolored.

## Character budgets

Measured, not guessed. Count before pasting.

| Title length | Result |
|---|---|
| Under 40 | Good. Reads at thumbnail size. |
| 40 to 65 | The working range. |
| 66 to 80 | Wraps to a third line. Rewrite. |
| Over 80 | Will be shrunk or truncated by whoever is in a hurry. Rewrite. |

If the template auto-shrinks text to fit, treat that as a warning rather than a feature. Auto-shrink is how a set of cards stops looking like a set.

## Writing the title

The OG title is a different job from the page title. The page title is for search and for someone already reading. The OG title is for someone scrolling past a link a colleague shared.

| Page H1 | OG title |
|---|---|
| "How We Approach Brand Strategy Engagements" | "Brand strategy, in six weeks not six months" |
| "Case Study: Northwind Logistics" | "Northwind cut quote turnaround from 5 days to 4 hours" |
| "Our Services" | "What we actually do, and what we charge" |

Three tests: it says something, it is legible at thumbnail size, and it would survive being read with no logo attached.

## When the template is the problem

Keep a short list of the times the template could not hold the content. When the same gap appears three times, fix the template once. Common ones: no room for a two-line title, no variant for case studies, no treatment for screenshots as opposed to photos, no dark variant.

Fixing the template is a design task with its own review. Detaching an instance on deadline is not a fix, it is the start of a second template nobody maintains.
