# Topic to asset

Each surviving topic becomes exactly one asset and gets one internal placement. One, because a topic split across a post, a video and a newsletter gets none of them finished.

## By cycle point

| Cycle point | Asset | Internal placement |
|---|---|---|
| `pre` | Public page or post aimed at the search or the scroll | Nothing. This one is for strangers. |
| `discovery` | Explainer with the firm's actual position in it | Pre-call send |
| `evaluation` | Comparison, worked example or case study with numbers | Follow-up email after call two |
| `proposal` | One-pager the team sends during a live deal | Proposal appendix, and the sales folder |
| `delivery` | How-to or process piece, published | Onboarding pack |
| `renewal` | Short written piece or a slide | Quarterly review deck |

The internal placement is the half everyone skips. An asset that exists but is not in the hands of the person on the call gets found by nobody, and next quarter the topic is still on the list.

## The brief

Four lines per topic. More is a content calendar; this is a plan.

```
TOPIC: Who owns the direction when the client has an in-house designer
Reach: 7 companies | Cycle: evaluation | Score: 14
Their words: "What happens when our designer disagrees with you?"
Asset: Worked example, how a shared-ownership engagement ran, with the
       two decisions that went the client's way
Placement: Follow-up after call two
Owner: [name] | Due: [date]
```

**Their words** is the mandatory line. It carries the buyer's phrasing into the brief so the finished piece uses the language they actually use rather than the firm's internal vocabulary.

## Formats that match

| The topic is | Write |
|---|---|
| A how-does-it-work question | A process piece with the real steps and the real timeline |
| A will-this-work-for-us question | A worked example with a comparable situation and numbers |
| A why-are-you-expensive objection | A piece on what the work involves, or a pricing page change. Not a values post. |
| A comparison to doing it in-house | An honest account of when in-house is the right answer |
| A misunderstanding of what the firm does | A positioning fix first. Content cannot outrun unclear positioning. |
| A delivery question asked repeatedly | A how-to, and a change to the onboarding pack |

## The quarterly cap

Four to six assets per quarter for a small agency doing client work. Plan more and the back half of the list does not ship, which costs more credibility than a shorter plan does.

Rank the full list, commit to the top five, and leave the rest visible as the reason the next quarter's plan already exists.
