# Extraction

One pass through the quarter, pulling units. No clustering, no theming, no deciding what matters. That comes after, and doing it during is what collapses a quarter of nuance into four generic topics.

## The unit

One question asked, or one objection raised, by someone who is not on your side of the table.

```
Q: "How do you handle it when our in-house designer disagrees with the direction?"
Company: 40-person logistics SaaS, Series A
Role: Head of Marketing
Call type: sales, second call
Cycle point: evaluation
Cost a deal: no
```

Six fields. The question verbatim, because the wording is the deliverable.

## What qualifies

- A direct question, whatever the answer was
- An objection, stated or hedged ("I'm not sure how our board would see that")
- A comparison to another vendor or to doing it in-house
- A request for proof, examples or references
- A misunderstanding of what you do, corrected on the call
- A question asked after signing, during delivery
- A question the buyer asks another person on their own side while you listen

That last one is the highest-signal unit there is and almost nobody logs it.

## What does not qualify

- Logistics: scheduling, invoicing, who is joining the next call
- Anything your side raised and the buyer merely agreed with
- Small talk, however charming
- A question you prompted by asking "do you have questions about X"
- Your own clever reframing of their question

The prompted-question rule removes a lot. Keep it anyway: a question the buyer would not have asked unprompted does not belong in a content plan built on what buyers actually want to know.

## Cycle points

Tag each unit with one. The tag decides what the topic becomes later.

| Tag | When |
|---|---|
| `pre` | Before or at the first call, often in the inbound message itself |
| `discovery` | First call, before anything is proposed |
| `evaluation` | Comparing, bringing in colleagues, asking for proof |
| `proposal` | Pricing, scope, terms, procurement |
| `delivery` | After signing |
| `renewal` | Expansion, renewal, or a second project |

## Volume

A quarter at a small agency is typically 40 to 80 calls, yielding 150 to 400 units. If you are getting under two units per call, the filter is too tight, most often because prompted questions are being dropped along with genuine ones asked in response to an open invitation.

## Keep the raw file

The unit list is the asset, not the topic list that comes out of it. Next quarter's comparison needs this quarter's units, not its conclusions, and a question that looked like a one-off in March is the third occurrence of something by September.
