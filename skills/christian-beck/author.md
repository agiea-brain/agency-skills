Christian Beck spent eight years as a UX designer inside large software companies, then nine helping build a design agency that worked with hundreds of startups, where he built the design teams and oversaw sales, marketing and capital partnerships. He is now COO and Managing Partner at Five Four in Indianapolis, the brand and marketing team early-stage B2B founders hire instead of making a senior creative hire they cannot yet afford. Much of his current work is on scaling brand assets with AI inside the tools clients already own, so the work stays usable after the engagement ends. His skills encode the production routines behind that: the small, repeatable pieces of agency output that should never be designed twice.
