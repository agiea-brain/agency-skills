Garrett Wolfe is the founder and GTM engineer behind OneGTM, a New York firm that builds outbound, signal and AI systems for startups. He was the first growth and bizops hire at Unify, where he built the automated outbound program from zero to more than $15M in pipeline within a year while the company grew past $6M ARR. Before that he co-founded Galaxy, sourced Series A and B software deals as a growth equity associate at TZP Group, and worked in Morgan Stanley's Global Technology Group. His skills cover the full outbound loop an agency runs for clients: scoring the TAM, writing the sequence, QA on what actually sent, and the performance review that keeps the client.
