# Evidence: Benchmarks

Vendor benchmarks define "reply rate" differently (per contact vs per email, all steps vs step 1),
so compare within a source, not across sources. Open rates are inflated by Apple MPP; prefer
reply- and meeting-measured findings. Pulled 2026-10-26.

## 1. Industry benchmarks

### Reply rates
| Metric | Value | Source (data period) |
|---|---|---|
| Avg reply rate | 3.43%; top 25% 5.5%; top 10% 10.7%+ | Instantly 2026 report (2025) |
| Reply per contact | median 0.74% (1 in 135); top 25% 1.43%; top 10% 2.63% | Smartlead State of Cold Email 2026 (Jan-Jun 2026, 850M+) |
| Avg reply rate | 4.5%; top campaigns 2-3 meetings per 100 emails | Saleshandy 2026 (30M) |
| Positive share of replies | ~14% (≈0.5% positive reply rate) | Something Inc. compilation, 2026 |
| Eric's bars | positive reply ≥1% good, ≥2% great; MMF ≥1 reply per 320 templated sends | GEX skills repo; Clay blog |

### Length
| Finding | Source |
|---|---|
| First emails under 50 words: 1.47% reply; 100-149 words need 97 contacts per reply vs 68 | Smartlead 2026 |
| 0-50 words +23% replies; grade 3-7 reading level 1.66x; mobile-optimized +24% | Lavender (vendor page) |
| 20-39 words best at 4.5%; gaps between buckets small | Hunter (2022-24, 34M) |
| Elite first touches average under 80 words | Instantly 2026 |
| 70-90 words best | Eric, internal review 2025 |
| 30-150 words beat ≤30 for meetings | Gong |

### Subject lines
| Finding | Source |
|---|---|
| 2-4 words: 46% open vs 34-39% at 7-10 words | Belkins (2024, 5.5M) |
| Personalized subject: **7% vs 3% reply** | Belkins (2024) |
| 5-7 words recommended | Saleshandy 2026 (disagrees) |

### CTAs
| Finding | Source |
|---|---|
| Interest CTA ("interested in learning more about X?") ~2x the next best cold | Gong Labs (2020, 304K) |
| Specific-time CTA books 15% cold vs 37% in active deals | Gong Labs |
| "Thoughts?" raises replies, cuts meetings 20%; "I never heard back" cuts meetings 14% | Gong |
| ROI claims −15% success | Gong |
| A single soft CTA: +78% positive replies vs hard CTAs | Saleshandy 2026 |

### Follow-ups
| Finding | Source |
|---|---|
| Per-step reply: E1 1.18%, E2 0.73%, E3 0.56%, E4 0.49%, E5 0.37% | Smartlead 2026 |
| Replies per 1,000 contacts: 12 (1 step), 25 (3), 33 (5), 40 (7) | Smartlead 2026 |
| 42% of replies from follow-ups; reply-style E2 +30% | Instantly 2026 |
| 57% of positive replies from follow-ups | Saleshandy 2026 |
| Step 3 = 35.6% of email-sourced meetings | Belkins (2025, 7.5M) |
| 4+ steps triple unsubscribe and spam; complaints 0.5% at E1 → 1.6% at E4 | Belkins (2024, 16.5M) |
| Reply share by step in Eric's sequences: ~35 / 30 / 20 / 15% | GEX skills repo |

### Segments and personalization
| Finding | Source |
|---|---|
| Campaigns under 200 prospects: 15-20% reply vs 8% at 500-1,000 | Saleshandy 2026 |
| Generic personalized variables: −6% reply, −26% interested | Reply.io |
| Creative-ideas campaign: 85 contacts per opportunity vs 176 for new-hire trigger | Eric (Instantly test, secondary) |
| Slot-defined AI ideas 21/23 usable vs free-form 2/5 | GEX `playbook-creative-ideas` |

### Timing
