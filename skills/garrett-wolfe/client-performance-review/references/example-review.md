# Acme Freight: three months in, and what comes next

_Fictional example · Draft for Dana · Data Aug 1 to Oct 25_

## 1. Summary

> **11 booked meetings, 3 open deals, and a clear answer on who buys.** Mid-size regional carriers reply; national carriers and middle management do not. Next quarter's target is 2+ meetings a week.

- **Reach:** 12,400 people at 6,100 accounts, by email and LinkedIn.
- **Biggest leak is at the bottom:** 24 people said "let's book a call"; 9 became a meeting. Closing that gap costs no extra sends.
- **Referrals beat every channel:** about 6x the interest rate of cold email, and 3 of the 11 meetings.
- **Top qualified objection:** "we're locked into a contract". A renewal-date play answers it.

## 3. How we get more meetings

<!-- table: widths=26,44,30 -->
| Lever | What we will do | Expected impact |
|---|---|---|
| Convert existing yeses | Same-day replies with a calendar link; chase all 15 open yeses to a booked call or a close | Highest, free. Half of yeses booking adds about 7 meetings |
| Tighter targeting | Weight lists to 50-500 staff and owner or ops contacts; drop national carriers from cold email | High. Same sends, segments that reply 2x as often |
| Ask for referrals | Ask every happy customer for one intro each quarter | High per intro; capped by customer count. Untested at volume |

## 4. Where we have not seen success

<!-- table: widths=30,22,48 -->
| Segment | Result | What we take from it |
|---|---|---|
| Carriers over 5,000 staff | 0 of 900 interested | Work them through referrals, not cold email |
| Owner-operators | 0 of 400 | Too small to need it; move them to DQ |
| Job-posting signal | 180 emailed, 0 replies | Signal fires wide; tighten the fit filter before it sends |

## 5. What we will improve

- **Reply speed and follow-through:** every positive reply answered the same business day, with one named owner chasing each yes.
- **Meeting tracking:** connect a calendar so meetings are counted automatically, not by confirmation.
- **One test per live campaign at a time** (opener, offer or schedule), so we keep learning without stopping anything.

## 6. New plays for next quarter
