# Slack message to the client team

Send it when the doc goes out (T-3 business days), in the shared client channel, from the account owner.
Share the doc as **Commenter** with everyone tagged before posting, and attach the Loom if there is one.

Tone follows `writing-rules.md` "Tone: honest and positive": open warm, put the wins and momentum first, frame each gap as upside with its fix.
Rules: under 200 words (renewal version) or 120 (routine); the link comes first; three findings at most, each with a number; ask for at most three decisions;
give a comment deadline and a call ask. No headers, and no bullets nested inside bullets. Edit it for AI patterns.

## Template

```
@<founder> @<others> our <period> review is ready: <doc link>

Short version: <one-line result, with the meeting and deal counts>.

Three things worth your eyes:
• <finding 1, with its number>
• <finding 2, with its number>
• <finding 3, with its number>

Where we need you (section 7): <ask 1>, <ask 2>, <ask 3>.

Comment anywhere in the doc by <day>. Then let's take 45 min on <day/time> to walk through your comments and the plan for next quarter.
```

## When the minimum term is ending

Open with the timing ("ahead of our 3-month minimum closing...") and add one block before the comment ask: up to 4 plays
we would run next, each tied to something the data showed (pull from §6 of the doc). Frame them as an invitation, not a pitch:
"If you're game to keep going, here's what we'd explore together:".

## Example (fictional client)

```
@Dana @Priya our three-month review is ready: <link>

Short version: 11 booked meetings and 3 open deals so far, and a clear picture of who buys.

Three things worth your eyes:
• 24 people said "let's book a call" and 9 became meetings. That's the cheapest gap to close
• Referral asks convert about 6x better than cold email (3 of the 11 meetings)
• "We're locked into a contract" is the top objection from qualified teams, which a renewal-date play answers

Where we need you (section 7): a second LinkedIn sender, one person who answers prospects within a day, and sign-off on the customer story.

Comment anywhere in the doc by Thursday. Then let's take 45 min Friday to walk through it and lock the plan.
```
