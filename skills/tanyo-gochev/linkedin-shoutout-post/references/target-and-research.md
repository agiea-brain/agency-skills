# Target screen and research checklist

## Target screen (all must pass)

| Check | Pass | Fail |
|---|---|---|
| Audience overlap | Their followers are the people you want reading your posts | Their audience is consumers, students, or a field you never sell into |
| Follower band | 10K to 100K | Under 10K (tag reaches too few) or a household name (your post is invisible to them) |
| One specific strength | You can finish "they are unusually good at ___" in under 10 words | You can only say "they're great" |
| Evidence | At least 3 linkable posts, pages or public numbers | Reputation only |
| History | You have never critiqued them in public and never shouted them out | Either one |

The target does not need to be a possible client. Many of the best ones never will be. What matters is that their audience is yours.

## Where to find targets

- Founders in your own feed whose posts you save.
- Active members of the communities your buyers are in.
- Guests on the podcasts your buyers listen to.
- Newsletters your buyers recommend to each other.
- People who keep showing up in the comments of the accounts your buyers follow.

## Research checklist

Content:
- [ ] Last 15 to 20 posts read. Note posting cadence and format mix (text, carousel, video, poll).
- [ ] Their best post of the last 90 days by comments, not likes. Note why: the hook, the question asked, the data shown.
- [ ] Do they reply to comments? How fast? Comment replies are often the strength nobody names.

Site and funnel:
- [ ] Home page: is it clear in 5 seconds who it is for and what to do next?
- [ ] Email capture, lead magnet, booking flow. Walk it as a buyer.

Newsletter:
- [ ] Subscribe. Note frequency, subject lines, and what the CTA asks for.
- [ ] Any visible growth tactic: referral program, cross-promotion, gated content.

Outbound:
- [ ] Only what you can see: a message you received, a public talk about their process. Do not infer.

Public record:
- [ ] Podcasts, talks, interviews. Any numbers they have shared about themselves.

Output of this step: 3 to 5 lines, each "what they do + the link that proves it + why it works".
