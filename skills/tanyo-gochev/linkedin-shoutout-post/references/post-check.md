# Post check (run before posting)

Every line must pass. One fail means rewrite.

| # | Check | Fail looks like |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Every claim has a source link in your notes | "They get great engagement" with nothing behind it |
| 2 | 3 to 5 patterns, each with what, evidence, why | One long paragraph of praise |
| 3 | The breakdown is 60 to 70% of the post | Half the post is about who they are |
| 4 | A reader could copy one pattern tomorrow | Patterns that only work because they are famous |
| 5 | Zero "but", "gap", "opportunity", "what I'd add" | Any critique, however soft |
| 6 | No mention of your offer, services, clients or a call link | "This is what we build for clients" |
| 7 | CTA is "follow them" | CTA points back at you |
| 8 | The tag is the right person and handle | Tagging the company page when the person did the work |
| 9 | Not a repeat target, not someone you critiqued | Same name in your content log |
| 10 | Would they screenshot and share it? | You hesitate when you answer |

## First comment check

- Goes one level deeper on one pattern from the post.
- Adds something new: a second example, a number, how to start doing it.
- No link to anything you sell.
