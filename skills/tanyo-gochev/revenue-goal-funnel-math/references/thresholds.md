# Planning defaults and alarm lines

For founder-led B2B services selling retainers through outbound. Replace each default with your own measured rate once a stage has 10 or more settled outcomes.

| Stage | Planning default | Alarm line | If below the line, the bottleneck is |
|---|---|---|---|
| Touches | Whatever the chain requires | Under the required weekly number | Volume: campaigns not running, lists not built, content not shipping |
| Reply rate (replies ÷ touches) | 5% blended (LinkedIn higher, cold email lower) | Under 3% | Message or list: wrong people, or the copy gives them no reason to answer |
| Reply → discovery call held | 15% | Under 10% | Booking friction: slow replies, no specific times offered, no-shows |
| Discovery → proposal | 50% | Under 40% | The first call: shallow discovery, unclear fit, no agreed next step |
| Proposal → close | 25% to 30% | Under 25% | The second call: generic proposal, weak value math, no momentum after |
| Close rate fine, revenue still short | | Value per client under plan | Price or positioning, not volume |

## Order of diagnosis

1. Start at touches and walk down.
2. Stop at the first stage below its alarm line. That is the bottleneck.
3. Ignore stages below it this week, even if they also look weak. Their numbers are too small to trust until the stage above is fixed.

## Sample-size rule

- Under 10 settled outcomes for a stage: use the default, mark it "assumed".
- 10 to 30: use your measured rate, mark it with n.
- Over 30: use the trailing 90 days, not lifetime. Old data hides recent change.
