# The funnel chain

Read top to bottom when planning. Read bottom to top when diagnosing.

```
Owner income per month
   = profit × owner's share
Profit per month
   = revenue × margin
Revenue per month
   = active clients × average monthly value per client
Active clients
   = last month's clients + closes − churn
Closes per month
   = proposals delivered × proposal-to-close rate
Proposals delivered
   = discovery calls held × discovery-to-proposal rate
Discovery calls held
   = replies × reply-to-call-held rate
Replies
   = touches × reply rate
Touches
   = sum of volume across channels
```

## Definitions that matter

| Term | Counts | Does not count |
|---|---|---|
| Touch | One message to one person on one channel | A post impression |
| Reply | Any human answer, positive or not | Auto-replies, out-of-office |
| Discovery call held | The first call actually took place | Booked but no-show |
| Proposal delivered | A tailored proposal walked through live or sent as a link | "I'll send something over" with nothing sent |
| Close | Signed and paid, or signed with a start date | A verbal yes |
| Settled | Closed or lost | Still open |

Proposal-to-close rate = signed ÷ settled. Leave open deals out of the denominator, and say so.

## Capacity check

After the chain, check the answer against capacity. If the goal needs more clients than the team can serve, the fix is price per client, not more outreach. Run the chain again at the higher price before adding volume.
