# Worked examples

Numbers are rounded for clarity.

## 1. Planning from an income goal (went to plan)

A two-founder services firm, profit split 50/50. One founder wants $20K a month in income.

- Profit needed: $20K ÷ 50% = $40K
- Revenue needed at 55% margin: $40K ÷ 0.55 ≈ $73K
- At $9K per client per month: about 8 active clients
- Starting from 2 clients, with about 1 churn a month: 3 closes a month nets +2. Three months to get there.

Per month, with measured and default rates:
- Closes: 3
- Proposals: 3 ÷ 30% = 10
- Discovery calls held: 10 ÷ 50% = 20
- Replies: 20 ÷ 15% ≈ 133
- Touches: 133 ÷ 5% ≈ 2,700

Per week: about 670 touches, 33 replies, 5 discovery calls, 2 to 3 proposals.

The weekly touch number is the line the founder takes into Monday. Everything above it is there to make that number believable.

## 2. Same close rate, opposite fixes (edge case)

Two firms each report 30 calls and 4 closes, a 13% close rate. Both want help with closing.

| | Firm A | Firm B |
|---|---|---|
| Discovery → proposal | 30 → 18 = 60% | 30 → 9 = 30% |
| Proposal → close | 18 → 4 = 22% | 9 → 4 = 44% |
| Bottleneck | The second call | The first call |
| Fix | Tailored proposal, stronger value math, a dated next step | Deeper discovery, a fit check, book the proposal call before hanging up |

Coaching Firm B on closing would make nothing better. Their closing is already strong. Only the split shows that.

## 3. The model that flattered (went wrong)

My own plan used a 25% close rate, because that is the common rule of thumb. Four weeks of real calls showed 2 closes from 15 calls held: 13%. Split into two stages, proposal-to-close came out near 30% (3 signed of 10 settled), so the leak sat partly in the first call too.

Re-running the chain with measured rates moved the weekly touch target from 400 to about 600. At the old target, every review would have said "on pace" while the pipeline fell short.

What to do instead: re-run the chain monthly with measured rates. When a rate drops, the touch target rises the same day, and the review says "behind" out loud.
