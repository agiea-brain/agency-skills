## Before and after

Cold email opener
- Before: "I wanted to reach out because we help B2B organizations leverage data-driven outbound methodologies to optimize pipeline generation." (one 20-word sentence)
- After: "We help B2B teams book more sales calls. We do it with cold email and LinkedIn." (two short sentences)

Follow-up
- Before: "Just circling back to see if you had the bandwidth to review the proposal prior to our next touch base."
- After: "Did you get time to read the proposal? Happy to walk you through it Thursday."

Landing page
- Before: "A holistic, scalable platform that empowers revenue teams to streamline their go-to-market motion."
- After: "One place to run your sales outreach. It grows with your team."

## When the rewrite turns childish

Grade 5 is about sentence shape and word length, not about hiding detail. If the rewrite lost the number, the name or the outcome, put it back.

- Too far: "We help you get more calls. It is easy."
- Right: "We booked 31 sales calls for a 12-person agency in 60 days."

The second one is grade 5 too. It just says something. (Use your own real numbers. Never invent one to make the line land.)
