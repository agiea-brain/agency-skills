# Check sheet

Attach these lines to every rewrite. All must pass.

| # | Check | Pass |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Average sentence length | 14 words or fewer |
| 2 | Longest sentence | 22 words or fewer |
| 3 | Ideas per sentence | One |
| 4 | Swap-list words left | Zero |
| 5 | Words of 3+ syllables | Only proper nouns, quotes, or terms the reader uses daily |
| 6 | Voice | Active throughout |
| 7 | Contractions | Used where natural |
| 8 | Semicolons | Zero |
| 9 | Numbers, names, claims from the original | All kept |
| 10 | Read aloud | No stumbles |
| 11 | Reading level estimate | Grade 5 or lower (any Flesch-Kincaid calculator) |

Format-specific:

| Format | Extra check |
|---|---|
| Cold email or first DM | Under 90 words |
| Post | Hook line passes on its own |
| Landing page | Headline and button text pass first |
| Call script | Every line can be said in one breath |
