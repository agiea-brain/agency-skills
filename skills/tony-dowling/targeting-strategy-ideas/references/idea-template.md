# Idea template and worked example

Output is chat-friendly and scannable on a phone. No preamble.

## Layout

**Lead line.** Name the target. If found in HubSpot, add deal stage and most recent activity. If call notes show prior contact, add one line on what was pitched and how it landed.

Then 3 to 4 numbered ideas:

```
Idea [N]: [Idea name]
• The Angle: why this matters to this specific business or persona right now.
• The Provocation: the commercial or liability consequence of doing nothing.
• Phone Opener Script: 2 to 3 sentences to secure a 15-minute diagnostic call.
```

Finish, every time:

```
⚠️ Pillar 2 Guardrail: Do not pitch features or pricing on this call. Your only objective is to secure the 15-minute Call 1.
```

## What each part must do

| Part | Must | Must not |
|---|---|---|
| Idea name | Name the commercial situation, 3 to 6 words | Name the service |
| The Angle | Tie a current pressure on this persona to one menu service | Describe the service |
| The Provocation | Name a specific cost of inaction: fine, lost tender, audit failure, churn, margin leak | Say "you could be losing money" |
| Phone Opener | Ask for 15 minutes and stop. Spoken rhythm, no jargon | Mention price, scope, or a feature |

## Worked example

Context: health and safety consultancy. Target: a regional construction contractor, 80 staff, bidding for main-contractor frameworks. Menu service used: retained competent-person support and pre-tender compliance review. Prior contact: none in the notes.

```
Idea 1: The framework pre-qualification gap
• The Angle: Main contractors are now scoring subcontractor safety management at pre-qualification, not just on site. A contractor of this size usually has the practice but not the evidence pack.
• The Provocation: Falling at pre-qualification does not show up as a lost deal. It shows up as a tender that never arrived, and nobody tells you why.
• Phone Opener Script: "I work with contractors your size who keep passing site audits and keep missing framework shortlists. There is usually one gap in the evidence pack causing it. Could I take 15 minutes to show you where it tends to be?"
```

Two or three more ideas follow the same shape, each anchored to a different menu line. Then the Guardrail.

## When the lookup fails

- Prospect not in HubSpot: say "Not found in HubSpot; angles built from the sector and the Menu for Sale." Then proceed.
- Menu missing: say "Menu for Sale not found in the client brain. Ideas withheld until it is located, because every angle must map to a sellable service." Do not produce ideas.
- Notes show an angle already rejected: drop it, and name the replacement angle as new.
