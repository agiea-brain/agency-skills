# /evaluate: scorecard and decision

Score from interview notes or a transcript. Every score carries a direct quote. A score with no quote is not a score.

## Dimensions

| Dimension | 1 to 3 | 4 to 6 | 7 to 10 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Commercial Maths | Could not give a weekly presentation number; claims did not multiply through | Gave numbers that roughly reconcile when pressed | Volunteered presentations, ratios and order value unprompted and they reconcile |
| Coachability | Defended all three critique points on the second roleplay | Changed one or two, explained the rest | Changed all three and asked what else |
| Hunting Ability | Best year was inbound or inherited; no cold origination story with a number | Some cold origination, mixed with marketing leads | Majority self-originated, with a method they can describe |
| Accountability Buy-in | Negotiated the list cap or the Friday review before an offer existed | Accepted, with reservations stated | Accepted and asked a practical question about how it runs |
| Low Drama | Blamed managers, territories or marketing for every miss | Owned some misses | Owned misses plainly and said what changed after |

## Layout

```
Scorecard
- Commercial Maths: [n]/10. "[quote]"
- Coachability: [n]/10. "[quote]"
- Hunting Ability: [n]/10. "[quote]"
- Accountability Buy-in: [n]/10. "[quote]"
- Low Drama: [n]/10. "[quote]"

Recommendation: **HIRE | CONDITIONAL | REJECT**
[reasoning, 3 to 6 lines]
```

## Decision rules

- Any dimension at 3 or below: not HIRE. Coachability or Accountability Buy-in at 3 or below: REJECT, because neither is trainable after the offer.
- All five at 7 or above: HIRE.
- Otherwise CONDITIONAL, with the condition named as something observable in the first 90 days (for example, 8 presentations a week by week 6, logged in the CRM) and written into the accountability agreement.

## Disqualifiers, regardless of total

- A quota claim that the candidate could not reconstruct and then restated as a different number.
- Refusal, in any wording, to be managed on the CRM record.
- Negotiating the Friday review out of the role before it is offered.
