# /contract: Pre-Employment Accountability Agreement

Generated from three inputs: candidate name, role, quota. Signature-ready. British English. No em dashes.

## Structure

```
PRE-EMPLOYMENT ACCOUNTABILITY AGREEMENT

Between: [Company], represented by [manager name, title]
And: [candidate name], for the role of [role]
Quota: [quota, with period]

Purpose
This agreement sets out what management will provide and what [candidate first name] agrees to deliver, so that accountability in the role rests on terms agreed before the role begins.

Part 1: Management Support Pledge (the 80%)
1.1 Menu for Sale: a maintained list of every service, its standard terms and what may be traded.
1.2 Comeback Book: the team's recorded, tested responses to the objections heard in market, with workshops to extend it.
1.3 CRM scaffolding: pipeline stages, deal properties and reports configured so that the record reflects reality with minimal effort.
1.4 Training: onboarding to the method, the Call 1 diagnostic, and the presentation standard.
1.5 Fair targets: a quota set from the firm's measured ratios, reviewed on the rolling 3-month horizon.

Part 2: Rep Execution Pledge (the 20%)
2.1 Presentation currency: [candidate first name] will put pricing in front of decision makers at the agreed weekly rate and will treat a presentation as the unit of work.
2.2 List rental: [candidate first name] holds up to 50 accounts on licence from the business. An account with no meaningful touch (a meeting booked or a brief taken) within the agreed window returns to the house pool.
2.3 CRM hygiene: every presentation, close date and stage change is logged the day it happens. The CRM record is the record.
2.4 Monday commitments: at the Monday 08:30 review, [candidate first name] commits to the week's Call 1s, presentations and cash asks.
2.5 Friday reality review: at the Friday 15:30 review, those commitments are reconciled against what happened, and the gap is discussed openly.

Part 3: Review
This agreement is reviewed at 90 days and then quarterly, alongside the quota.

Signed: ______________________  [candidate name]   Date: ________
Signed: ______________________  [manager name]     Date: ________
```

## Personalisation

- Replace every bracket. The candidate's first name appears in Part 2 so the pledges read as theirs.
- Where the /evaluate recommendation was CONDITIONAL, add the condition as clause 2.6 with its measurable and its date.
- Where the client workspace documents a different cap, window or review time, use the client's figures and say so above the agreement.

## What it is for

The rep who signs this cannot, six months in, say Friday was a surprise. The manager who signs it cannot withhold the list, the book or the CRM and then blame the rep. Both halves are the point.
