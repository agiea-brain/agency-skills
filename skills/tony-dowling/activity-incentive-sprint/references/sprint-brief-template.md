# Executive Sprint Brief

Create it as a shared document named for the sprint. Keep it to one page.

## Sections, in order

1. **Sprint Name and Mission.** A name the team will say out loud, and one sentence on the behaviour being driven and why now.
2. **Timeframe and Scope.** Format (1-day Blitz, 3-day Push, 2-week Sprint), start and end, who is in.
3. **Primary and Secondary Metrics with Targets.** The workings, per rep and team total.
4. **Qualifying Criteria.** Exactly what counts as a valid point.
5. **Live Scoreboard Mechanism.** Where the tally lives and who updates it, and when.
6. **The Reward and Celebration.** What, when, and how it is marked.
7. **Daily Leaderboard Prompts.** Three huddle talking points.

## Format by window

| Window | Format | Use when |
|---|---|---|
| 1 day | Blitz | One list to clear, one behaviour, everyone in the room |
| 2 to 3 days | Push | Call 1 bookings or presentations have dropped and the month needs them back |
| 1 to 2 weeks | Sprint | Uncovering new decision makers across the book, or a quarter-opening reset |

Never longer than two weeks.

## Target workings

```
Baseline per rep per week   × reps × weeks in window  = expected
Expected × 1.4 to 1.6                                   = sprint target (team)
Sprint target ÷ reps                                    = per-rep target
```

Example: baseline 4 Call 1s a week per rep, 5 reps, 3-day Push (0.6 of a week). Expected 4 × 5 × 0.6 = 12. Target 18, a 50% lift. Per rep: 4 in three days, with the first to 4 unlocking the spot prize.

Write the line in the brief exactly like this: "baseline 4 Call 1s/week per rep × 5 reps = 20 a week; sprint target 18 across 3 days, a 50% lift on the run rate".

## Qualifying criteria that hold up

| Metric | Counts when | Does not count when |
|---|---|---|
| Call 1 Booked | Booked AND logged in the CRM with a future date and a named decision maker | Verbal "yes, call me", a placeholder with no date, an internal meeting |
| Presentation Made | A proposal or pricing was shared and the deal stage reflects it | A capabilities chat, a quote mentioned but not sent |
| Meaningful conversation | A two-way conversation with a decision maker that ended in a next step | Voicemail, gatekeeper, "send me something" with no follow-up agreed |
| New decision maker identified | Named C-suite or budget holder added to the CRM with a verified role | A generic info@ address, a contact already in the record |

## Reward patterns

- Spot prize on the day for the first to hit the per-rep number.
- Team experience (lunch out, an afternoon off, an early Friday finish) when the team total lands.
- Public recognition at the Friday review: the top three named, the behaviour described.

Avoid anything that pays weeks later or needs a calculation nobody can do in their head.

## Daily huddle prompts

1. **Callout (morning, day 1):** name yesterday's baseline and today's number. "Four each by Thursday close. Who is going first?"
2. **Momentum nudge (mid-sprint):** read the tally aloud, name who is within one of the number, ask what each person's next three calls are.
3. **Closing push (last afternoon):** "Two hours left. The board says 15 of 18. Three bookings in the room settles it. Phones up."
