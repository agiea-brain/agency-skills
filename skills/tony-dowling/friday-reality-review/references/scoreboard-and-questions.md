# Friday Wrap Scoreboard

## Table

Ranked by category (Blocked, then At Risk, then Delivered) and within category by value at risk.

```
| Rep | Won / Lost (n, £) | Presentations | Call 1s held / booked | vs target | Category |
```

Roll-up line beneath the team table: totals for each column, and the count of reps in each category.

## What slipped and why

One block per At Risk or Blocked rep. Each slipped deal gets a line:

```
[Deal name] · £[amount] · close date [old] → [new] · [reason from notes, in the prospect's or rep's words] · [HubSpot stage, flagged if it disagrees with the note]
```

Reasons worth naming: a named blocker inside the account, a competitor mentioned, a budget freeze, a champion gone quiet, a proposal not yet sent, a close date that was never real.

## Categories

| Category | Condition | What the one-to-one is for |
|---|---|---|
| Delivered | Met or exceeded Monday's commitments | Lock in what worked; set next week's number |
| At Risk | Slippage on one or more deals, pipeline pushed, commitments missed without an external cause | Find the real reason; reset the plan for the slipped deals |
| Blocked | A genuine external blocker the rep cannot clear alone | Agree the management intervention and who makes it |
| No activity | Nothing logged, nothing in the notes | Establish whether the week happened off-system or did not happen |

## Coaching questions

Three per rep, specific to their line. Patterns, with the data each one needs:

| Pattern | Needs | Example |
|---|---|---|
| The pushed date | A deal with a moved close date | "The [account] close moved to the 14th. What did they say that made the 14th more real than the 7th?" |
| The silent deal | A deal with a close date this week and no activity | "[Account] was due to close this week and there is nothing logged since Tuesday. Where is it really?" |
| The missing ask | Presentations below commitment | "You committed to three presentations and made one. Which two conversations got to the money and stopped?" |
| The no-show | Call 1s booked above Call 1s held | "Two of five Call 1s did not happen. Were they confirmed the day before?" |
| The blocker | A Blocked category | "What do you need from me on [account], and by when?" |
| The clean slate | Any rep, last question | "What is the one thing you want off your desk before Monday?" |

Avoid: "how was your week", "what are you going to do differently", anything answerable with "fine".

## Delivery by audience

| Audience | Gets |
|---|---|
| Individual rep | Own line, own slipped deals, own three questions |
| Sales manager | Full team table, roll-up, At Risk and Blocked list, every rep's questions |
| The team | Team table with headline numbers and categories only |

Primary: on screen, then a dated "Friday Reality Review" document in the client brain, manager view as the main file with a section per rep.
