# The note

Two or three sentences. Says why it made you think of them. Asks for nothing.

## Shape

```
[The specific reason, referencing something of theirs.]
[One line on what the piece says, in your words.]
[Link.]
```

No greeting beyond their name, no sign-off beyond yours. Anything longer starts to look like an email you wanted to send anyway.

## Examples

**From something they said**

> Sam, you mentioned the Mexico lane tariff exposure back in March. This is the first thing I've read that puts a number on it.
> [link]

**A person they know**

> Dana, your old boss at Tessera is quoted halfway down this. Thought you'd want to see it before someone forwards it to you.
> [link]

**One step outside their industry**

> Mark, this is about what's happening to their customers rather than to them, which I suspect is the part that actually hits your pipeline next year.
> [link]

**The non-work one**

> Hey, this is just a good piece about the Leafs' goaltending problem. No business content whatsoever.
> [link]

**Lapsed contact**

> Priya, it's been far too long. Saw this on the lab-grown side and remembered you were sceptical of the whole category two years ago. Curious whether you still are.
> [link]

That last one carries a question, which is allowed because it is about the article. The forbidden thing is an ask about you.

## Never in these notes

- "Hope you're well" and anything else that fills a line
- "Thought you'd find this interesting". Says nothing, and signals a template
- Any ask: a meeting, a call, a reply, an introduction
- Your product, your service, your availability
- A pasted paragraph from the article. Link to it, say one line about it
- A summary longer than the note
- More than one article in a single note

## Length

Under 50 words. Over that, the recipient reads it as an email rather than a passing thought, and the whole premise is that it was a passing thought.

## The copyright line

Link, never paste. One short quoted line at most, in quotation marks, and only when the exact wording is the reason for sending. The recipient either has a subscription or does not; forwarding the full text of a piece you pay for is not a favour you are entitled to do.

## The review step

Every note is read before it goes. Three checks:

1. Could this be sent to anyone else in the file without edits? Then it is not specific enough.
2. Is there an ask hiding in it, including an implied one?
3. Would it read as odd if they had not heard from you in six months?

The edits made at this step are the most valuable training signal the system produces, which is what the feedback log is for.
