Andrew Kappel runs LeaderLev Consulting, where he works with complex B2B businesses on sales and CRM: benchmarking where they are, defining what good would look like, and then getting it built, with more deals, better conversion and shorter cycles as the measure. He is also COO of Trust2Connect, a business verification startup automating onboarding and monitoring for financial institutions that serve small businesses. A two-time podcaster, marathoner and former MLB employee, his skills encode the daily habits behind a relationship-led pipeline rather than a campaign-led one.
