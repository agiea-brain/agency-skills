Robert Kaminski co-founded Fletch PMM, a product marketing practice working with early-stage B2B SaaS companies on positioning and messaging. His work focuses on the gap between what a product does and how its buyers describe the problem it solves, and on reading sales conversations as market evidence rather than as pipeline notes.
