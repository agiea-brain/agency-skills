Paul Munro coaches the frontline sales managers at growth-stage B2B software companies, one manager at a time, on the argument that the best seller on the floor gets handed a team and is never taught the job. He led Clio's largest sales segment across five managers and around fifty reps, was VP of Sales at Intiveo and Head of Sales at EvenUp, and founded Applied GTM Strategies in 2024 to do this work independently. His skills encode how he runs a coaching engagement: what he walks into each session with, and how he holds a client to what they said they would do.
