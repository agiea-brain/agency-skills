Paul Munro coaches the frontline sales managers at growing B2B companies, one manager at a time, on the argument that the best seller on the floor gets handed a team and is never taught the job. Before that: Salesforce, then Clio, where he ran sales from about $50M through the $100M mark, then VP of Sales at Intiveo, then Head of Sales at EvenUp, where he grew ARR from $10M to $28M in nine months and the team from 4 AEs to 35 people. He founded Applied GTM Strategies in 2024 to do this work independently. His skills encode how he runs a coaching engagement: what he walks into each session with, and how every deal movement gets recorded.
