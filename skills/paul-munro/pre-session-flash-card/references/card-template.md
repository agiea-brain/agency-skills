# The card

One page. Fixed order. Fill every field or write "none on file"; a blank field reads as an oversight, "none on file" reads as a finding.

```
CLIENT: [name]          SESSION: [n]          DATE: [today]
ENGAGEMENT GOAL: [one line, unchanged week to week]

OPENER
[One question. Specific, references last session, cannot be answered "good".]

LAST PREDICTION                                      [made: date | due: date]
They said: "[verbatim]"
What happened: [hit / missed / unresolved] - [one line of evidence]
[If missed and unmentioned: FLAG - raise in first 5 minutes]

LISTEN FOR
1. [pattern]
2. [pattern]
3. [pattern]

SINCE LAST TIME
[One line: what they sent, or what they went quiet on.]

NEXT PREDICTION                                       [captured at session end]
[blank until the call closes]
```

## Field notes

**Engagement goal** does not change week to week. If you find yourself rewriting it, the engagement has drifted and that is this session's real subject.

**Opener** is one question. The temptation is three. Three openers means you have not decided what matters, and the client will answer the easiest one.

**Last prediction** is quoted, not paraphrased. Paraphrasing softens it, and a softened prediction cannot be missed. "What happened" is one line of evidence, not your interpretation.

**Listen for** is exactly three. At two you are under-prepared, at four you stop listening and start checking boxes.

**Since last time** is often the most useful line on the card and takes ten seconds to write. Silence counts as an entry.

**Next prediction** stays blank until the call closes. Prefilling it means you have decided their commitment for them, and they will agree to it without meaning it.

## Timing

Built in under ten minutes. If it takes longer, the client folder is the problem, not the card: last session's write-up was too long or too vague to be used as an input.

Read in under two minutes, immediately before the call, not the night before.
