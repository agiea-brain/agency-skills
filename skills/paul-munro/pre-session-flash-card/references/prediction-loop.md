# The prediction loop

The card only works because every session ends with one. This page is how predictions are captured, what makes a usable one, and what a miss means.

## What a prediction is

The client, in their own words, states a concrete outcome and the date it will be true by. Not a task list. Not an intention.

| Not a prediction | A prediction |
|---|---|
| "I'll work on the pricing" | "I'll have sent the new rate to the two retainer clients by the 14th" |
| "We need to hire" | "The job post is live and three candidates have applied by month end" |
| "I'll think about dropping that client" | "I'll have told them by Friday, or decided to keep them and stopped bringing it up" |

Three tests. If any fails, it is not usable and you fix it before the call ends.

1. **Binary.** On the due date, hit or missed, with no argument about which.
2. **Dated.** Before the next session, or explicitly flagged as a multi-week prediction.
3. **Theirs.** They said the words. A prediction you supplied and they agreed to is yours, and they will not feel the miss.

## Capturing it

Last five minutes of the session, not the last thirty seconds. Ask: "What will be true by the time we next talk, that is not true now?"

Write their answer verbatim into the client folder while still on the call. Read it back. The read-back is where the vague ones get caught, because people hear their own hedging when it is said out loud.

## Scoring it

| Outcome | What it means | What the next prediction does |
|---|---|---|
| Hit, comfortably | They were predicting inside what they had already decided to do | Push harder. One size up. |
| Hit, barely | Correctly calibrated. This is the target. | Same size. |
| Missed, obstacle they named | The obstacle is the work. | Prediction targets the obstacle, not the original outcome. |
| Missed, no obstacle | Avoidance. Something about the task is uncomfortable. | Smaller prediction, sooner date, until the pattern breaks. |
| Missed, unmentioned by them | The most important signal on the card. | Nothing, until you have talked about why it went unmentioned. |
| Unresolved | The prediction failed the binary test. | Your error, not theirs. Say so, and fix the format. |

## Patterns across sessions

Keep the last six on file. They read as a record of the engagement that no write-up gives you.

- **Six hits in a row**: predictions are too small. The client is managing you.
- **Three misses in a row, same theme**: the engagement is pointed at the wrong constraint. Stop and rescope.
- **Misses that cluster on anything involving a conversation with another person**: the work is not strategy, it is the conversation. Rehearse it in session.
- **Predictions getting longer and more conditional**: they are hedging against being held to them. Shorten the horizon.
