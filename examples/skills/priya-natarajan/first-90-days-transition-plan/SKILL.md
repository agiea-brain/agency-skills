Applies in week 1 or 2 of a first VP role. Produces a one-page 90-day plan the client writes themselves.

## The play

1. Run a 90-minute first session. Never write the plan for the client; they write it in the session.
2. Have them list everything they did in their last role. Sort each item into keep, delegate, or stop. Keep list must be under 30% of old tasks. Most clients start near 70%.
3. Build the stakeholder map: boss, peers, directs, skip-levels, one external. For each, record what they need from the client, what the client needs from them, and current trust 1 to 5.
4. Ask the four questions in references/session-script.md before drafting outcomes.
5. Write outcomes for days 1-30, 31-60, 61-90 using the rules in references/phase-rules.md.
6. Branch by how they got the role:
   - Internal promotion: the old team still comes to them, so the stop list is larger. Add a handoff conversation script for each direct who keeps coming back.
   - External hire: weeks 1-3 are listening tours. All days 1-30 outcomes are "learn", none are "deliver".
   - More than 8 directs: add a span-of-control conversation with their boss inside the first 30 days.
7. Share the one-pager with their boss within the first 30 days. The plan is a trust-building artifact as much as a plan.
8. Check in every two weeks, 45 minutes. Open references/worked-cases.md when the client pushes back on the stop list or the plan stalls.

## What good looks like

- First thing I check that most people skip: the client's week-1 calendar. If it is full of meetings they attended before the promotion, they have not transitioned, whatever they say.
- The mediocre version: a generic 30/60/90 with ten goals per phase, all delivery goals, no stop-doing list, no trust scores. It looks productive and changes nothing.
- I know the plan is right when the client can name, for each phase, the one thing their boss will notice. And when the stop list has at least one item that scares them.
- I know a session landed when they cancel a recurring meeting from their old role within a week.
- Red flag: at day 45 they still attend their old team's standup. Have a direct conversation.

## Rules

- MUST cap each 30-day phase at three outcomes, with at least one a relationship outcome.
- MUST record a trust score for every stakeholder.
- MUST treat boss trust under 3 as the only real outcome of days 1-30.
- MUST make the day-60 outcome a visible win on something the boss said they cared about in week 1.
- MUST make the day-90 outcome a decision their predecessor would not have made.
- NEVER accept "nobody else can do it" for a kept task. Ask "then who are you hiring or developing by day 60".
- NEVER write the plan for the client.
