# First-session script

90 minutes. The client writes; the coach asks.

| Step | Time | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 25 min | Old-role inventory, then keep/delegate/stop sort |
| 2 | 25 min | Stakeholder map with trust 1-5 |
| 3 | 30 min | Four questions, then 30/60/90 outcomes |
| 4 | 10 min | Pick the first meeting to cancel; schedule the boss share |

## The four questions

| Question | What it listens for |
|---|---|
| What did your boss say they needed when they offered you the job? | The day-60 win. Vague answer means the boss conversation has not happened yet. |
| Who on your team is going to be most upset you got this role? | The internal candidate who was passed over; first handoff or retention risk. |
| What are you still doing that a VP shouldn't? | Whether the stop list will scare them. A safe answer means keep digging. |
| Who do you not have a relationship with yet that can block you? | Hidden stakeholders for the map; low-trust entries. |

## Handoff conversation (internal promotions)

For each direct who still brings old-role work: name the decision that is now theirs, name what the client still wants to hear about, set a date when escalation stops.
