# Worked cases

## Went to plan: internal promotion, 12 directs

Engineering manager promoted to VP Eng. Keep list started at 80%. Cut to 25% by delegating all code review and on-call escalation. Day-60 win: shipped a hiring plan the CEO had asked for twice. Span-of-control conversation with the CEO happened in the first 30 days.

## Edge: external hire, CEO still runs the function

External VP Sales hired into a company whose CEO was also running sales. Map showed CEO trust at 2; his need from the VP: "proof you won't lose his accounts". Days 1-30 became entirely riding along on his accounts.

## Went wrong: kept 60% of old tasks

VP Marketing kept 60% because "nobody else can do the brand work". By day 45 her team was leaderless on everything else. The plan had to be restarted. The coach's mistake: accepting "nobody else can" instead of asking "then who are you hiring by day 60".
