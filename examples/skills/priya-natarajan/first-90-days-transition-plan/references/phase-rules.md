# Phase rules and thresholds

| Phase | Max outcomes | Required shape | Test |
|---|---|---|---|
| Days 1-30 | 3 | At least one relationship outcome. External hires: all "learn". If boss trust is under 3, that is the only real outcome. | Client names the one thing the boss will notice |
| Days 31-60 | 3 | First visible win on something the boss named in week 1 | Boss can say what changed |
| Days 61-90 | 3 | A decision their predecessor would not have made | Client can say why the predecessor would have decided differently |

| Check | Threshold | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Keep list | Under 30% of old tasks | Above it, push tasks to delegate or stop |
| Stakeholder trust | 1-5 per person; under 3 with boss | Relationship outcome becomes the core of days 1-30 |
| Directs | More than 8 | Span-of-control conversation with boss in first 30 days |
| Check-ins | Every 2 weeks, 45 min | Skip none in the first 60 days |
| Day 45 | Still in old team's standup | Direct conversation |

Framework note: this bends Watkins' First 90 Days. Drop the STARS model; use the keep/delegate/stop sort, and require trust numbers.
