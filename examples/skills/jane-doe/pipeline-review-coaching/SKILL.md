Applies before any recurring pipeline review. Produces a coaching agenda and a forecast call.

## The play

1. Pull the open pipeline and sort by close date, nearest first.
2. For each deal inside the quarter, read the last two activities and the next step.
3. Score each deal with the scorecard in references/scorecard.md.
4. Write one coaching question per deal that forces the rep to name the risk.

## What good looks like

- The best coach notices deals with no next step before looking at amount.
- The mediocre version reads stages aloud and asks "anything else?"
- Good output changes at least one close date or amount in the forecast.

## Rules

- MUST produce a forecast adjustment, even if it is zero.
- NEVER let a deal stay in commit without a dated next step.
